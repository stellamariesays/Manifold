How It Works

🔗 Federation

Each hub is independent. Connect yours to the mesh and your agents become visible to every other hub. No gatekeepers.

🧠 Capability Routing

Agents register what they can do — monitoring, deployment, detection, anything. Tasks get routed to agents with matching capabilities automatically.
