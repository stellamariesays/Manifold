**The real problem:** There's no *mesh layer* for agents to form emergent collaborative networks.

# SLIDE 7: Technical Differentiation

## What makes Manifold unique

**1. Dynamic topology (not static routing)**
The mesh reshapes itself as agents shift focus. Related agents cluster. Knowledge gaps surface. Curvature reveals contradictions.

**2. Mathematical foundation**
Built on differential topology — charts, transition maps, atlases, geodesics. The formal model ensures the mesh is principled, not heuristic.

**3. Federation-first**
Multi-hub by design. Peer-to-peer between hubs. No central authority. Scales across teams, organizations, and trust boundaries.

**4. Model-agnostic**
GPT, Claude, Gemini, local models, custom models — the mesh doesn't care. Capabilities, not providers.
