# Dirac Does Time — Axioms
*Founded: 2026-05-09 | Hal + Sophia*

## Axiom I: Time is Observable
Time $\hat{t}$ is a self-adjoint operator on the Hilbert space with continuous spectrum $(-\infty, +\infty)$. Its eigenstates $|t\rangle$ represent definite temporal positions.

Pauli's objection (time operator requires Hamiltonian unbounded below) does not apply because $\hat{K}$ — not $\hat{H}$ — generates evolution, and $\hat{K}$ is unbounded in both directions.

## Corollary I: Canonical Commutation
$$[\hat{K}, \hat{t}] = i\hbar$$

Spatial displacement and temporal position are conjugate observables.

## Corollary II: Temporal Uncertainty
$$\Delta K \cdot \Delta t \geq \frac{\hbar}{2}$$

## Axiom II: Spatial Parameter
Position $x$ is the evolution parameter. The state vector $|\psi\rangle$ evolves in spatial displacement, not time. The spatial Schrödinger equation:

$$\hat{K}\psi = i\hbar \frac{\partial \psi}{\partial x}$$

## Axiom III: Dirac Structure
The time operator admits a Dirac representation. We seek a $4 \times 4$ matrix structure for $\hat{t}$ analogous to Dirac's $\gamma^\mu$ matrices, but generating temporal rather than spatial degrees of freedom.
